# UAE government fees for businesses

297 fees and fines that UAE authorities publish for companies, visas and licences: GDRFA, ICP, the
Federal Tax Authority, Dubai's economy department, free zones such as RAKEZ and DMCC, the Dubai Health Authority
and more. Every row names the authority, links the official page the figure comes from and gives the date it was
last checked there (latest check: 2026-11-12).

Maintained by [SwiftHub Business Consultants](https://swifthub.ae/), a company formation consultancy in Dubai. The
figures are the ones quoted across swifthub.ae, so each row also links the guide that explains the fee in context.
Browse and search them on one page: [UAE government fees 2026](https://swifthub.ae/uae-government-fees/).

- `uae-government-fees.csv` and `uae-government-fees.json`: the same data in two formats.
- Columns: `id`, `fee`, `amount_aed`, `authority`, `publisher_detail`, `source_url`, `checked_on`, `explained_at`.

Fees change. Always confirm on the authority's own page (the `source_url`) before you pay or quote one. This is a
reference list, not legal or tax advice. Corrections are welcome as issues or pull requests.
